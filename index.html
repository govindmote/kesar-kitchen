Chef Meera Kulkarni

Meera learned to cook beside her grandmother in Kolhapur, grinding spices on a stone before sunrise. After ten years in kitchens in Mumbai and Lisbon, she came home to Pune with one idea: keep the flavours of the family table and serve them with care.

Her menu changes with the market. If the tomatoes are sweet, they’re on the plate. If they aren’t, they wait.

Meera Kulkarni, Head Chef
